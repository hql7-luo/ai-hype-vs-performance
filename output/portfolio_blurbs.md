# Portfolio project copy

## English

AI Talk vs. AI Results is an independent R data-science project linking 3,910 real SEC 10-K filings from 498 current S&P 500 issuers to later revenue growth and stock returns. I built a cached EDGAR pipeline, a transparent AI dictionary, a Quarto report, and a Shiny explorer. In the main industry/year model with size and prior-growth controls, one additional mention per 10,000 words is associated with 0.981 percentage points of next-FY revenue growth and 1.273 percentage points of 12-month return above SPY. The return slope changes to -0.242 percentage points with firm effects (p=0.708) and 0.729 with winsorization (p=0.074), so the report presents specification sensitivity alongside survivorship and measurement limits and treats all estimates as correlations.

## 中文

AI Talk vs. AI Results 是一个独立完成的 R 语言数据科学项目，将 498 家当前标普 500 成分公司的 3,910 份真实 SEC 10-K 年报，与后续营收增长和股价表现连接起来。我搭建了可缓存的 EDGAR 数据流程、透明可修改的 AI 词典、Quarto 报告和 Shiny 交互应用。在控制行业、年份、营收规模和当期增速的主模型中，每万词增加一次 AI 提及，与下一财年营收增速变化 0.981 个百分点、12 个月相对 SPY 收益变化 1.273 个百分点相关。收益斜率在加入公司固定效应后为 -0.242 个百分点（p=0.708），在缩尾处理后为 0.729 个百分点（p=0.074），因此报告明确呈现模型敏感性、幸存者偏差和测量局限，并将全部结果限定为相关性。
